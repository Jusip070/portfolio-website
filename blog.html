Untied Dog or Confined in a Cage

Posted on November 14, 2026

Blog Description

This blog discusses the idea of freedom versus restriction. It compares an untied dog that can move freely with a dog confined in a cage, highlighting how freedom allows growth, happiness, and choice, while confinement limits potential and expression.

R.A. No. 9482 “Anti-Rabies Act of 2007” states that pet dogs are prohibited from scattering, and whoever violates this law will be fined in the amount of 500 pesos. R.A. No. 8485 “Animal Welfare Act of 1998” prohibits killing or torturing dogs without sufficient cause.

As human beings, we need discipline in caring for animals. Let us tie them properly or not allow them to get out of our yard to avoid accidents and ensure safety for everyone.
